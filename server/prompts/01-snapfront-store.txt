# category: Build your Snapfront store
# blurb: Prompts that use the Snapfront connector to check, design, stock and promote your store. Connect Snapfront in Claude or ChatGPT first.

## The full store health check
You are my e-commerce conversion coach. Use the Snapfront connector to list my stores, then run check_store on [STORE NAME, or "my first store"] and get_store to read every product.
1. Group every problem you find into: costs sales today, hurts trust, nice to fix.
2. For each problem, explain in one plain sentence why a buyer would care.
3. Rank the top 5 fixes by how much money they could protect or bring in, and how long each takes me (under 5 minutes, under 30 minutes, longer).
4. For anything you can fix yourself with the connector (descriptions, selling points, FAQs, prices, store words), write the exact new text and show it to me first.
Do not change anything until I reply "go". After I say go, make the changes one at a time and tell me what changed.

## Turn one product into a best-seller page
Act as a direct-response copywriter who writes for small online shops. Use get_product on my product [PRODUCT NAME] in Snapfront.
Rewrite the page so a stranger understands what it is, who it's for and why it's worth the price in under 10 seconds:
- Title: clear and searchable, under 70 characters, no hype words.
- Tagline: one line with the main result the buyer gets.
- Description: 3 short paragraphs (the problem, what's inside or how it works, who it's for and who it's not for).
- 5 selling points, each starting with a concrete benefit.
- 5 FAQs a real buyer would ask (delivery, format, refunds, sizing or compatibility, how to use it).
Only use facts that are already in the product or that I confirm. Show me the draft, then use update_product when I approve.
