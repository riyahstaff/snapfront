# category: Career and income growth
# blurb: Raises, job search, new skills and career moves that grow your main income alongside your store.

## Ask for a raise with evidence
You are a salary negotiation coach. My role is [ROLE] at [COMPANY TYPE], I've been there [TIME], and my wins this year are [WINS, WITH NUMBERS IF POSSIBLE].
Help me build my case: my impact in business terms, how to research market pay (which sources to check), the number to ask for and my walk-away number.
Write the meeting request email, a 2-minute opening script, answers to 5 likely pushbacks ("there's no budget", "wait until review season", "everyone got the same raise") and a follow-up email confirming what was agreed.

## Negotiate a job offer
Act as an offer negotiation coach. My offer is: [SALARY, BONUS, BENEFITS, START DATE, REMOTE DAYS, TITLE]. I want [WHAT YOU WANT] and my other options are [OTHER OFFERS OR NONE].
Help me decide what to ask for and in what order. Write a warm, confident counteroffer email and a phone script.
Tell me what else is negotiable if salary is fixed (a signing bonus, title, paid time off, start date, a learning budget, remote days) and how to respond if they say no.

## Tailor my resume to a job
You are a professional resume writer. Here's the job post: [PASTE] and my resume: [PASTE].
Rewrite my bullet points to match the job's top requirements using only my real experience. Start each bullet with a strong verb and add results with numbers where I've given them. Write a 3-line summary for the top.
List the keywords from the job post I'm missing, which ones I can honestly add, and tell me straight whether I'm a strong fit.

## Cover letter that doesn't sound generic
Act as a hiring manager who reads hundreds of cover letters. Write my cover letter for [JOB] at [COMPANY].
Open with why this company and this role specifically, tell one short story that proves I can do the hardest part of the job, connect my experience [KEY EXPERIENCE] to their needs, and close with confidence. Under 250 words.
Ask me for one specific thing I like about the company if I haven't told you, and never invent experience.

## Interview prep with practice questions
You are an interview coach. I'm interviewing for [ROLE] at [COMPANY TYPE]. Here's my key experience: [EXPERIENCE].
Give me the 10 most likely questions, including 4 behavioral ones, and a strong answer outline for each using the STAR method (situation, task, action, result) and my real experience.
Then interview me one question at a time. After each answer, give me feedback on clarity, length and impact, and a better version.

## Networking messages that get replies
Act as a networking coach. Write 5 short, specific messages: reaching out to someone in a role I want, asking for a 15-minute chat, following up after the chat with thanks and one takeaway, reconnecting with an old coworker, and asking for a referral to a specific job.
Each should be under 80 words, respectful of their time and easy to say yes to. Add tips on who to contact first and how many messages to send each week.
