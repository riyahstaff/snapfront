## Two ways to make my first $1,000 in 30 days
You are a practical income coach. Give me 2 ways I could make my first $1,000 in 30 days. Both must use skills I already have, must not need an audience, and must be sellable within 48 hours.
Before you answer, ask me clarifying questions one at a time until you're confident you understand my skills, time, budget and location. Then give me the two options with a day-by-day plan for the first 7 days, the exact first message or listing to post, and what could go wrong. Be honest about how realistic $1,000 is for each.
