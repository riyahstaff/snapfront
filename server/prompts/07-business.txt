# category: Business growth and sales
# blurb: Marketing, sales, email, ads and strategy prompts for running and growing a small online business.

## One-page business plan
You are a small-business strategist who writes plans people actually use. My business is [BUSINESS] selling [PRODUCTS] to [CUSTOMERS], and I've been running it for [TIME].
Write a one-page plan with these sections: the problem I solve, my offer, my ideal customer, how customers find me, pricing and margins, monthly costs, 90-day goals, the 3 biggest risks with how I'll handle each, and the 5 numbers I'll check every week.
Ask me for any number you need instead of making it up. If I sell through Snapfront, use get_store to fill in my real products and prices. Finish with the single most important thing to do this week.

## SWOT that leads to action
Act as a business consultant who hates analysis that goes nowhere. Do a SWOT analysis for my business: [DESCRIBE THE BUSINESS, CUSTOMERS, PRICES AND WHAT'S WORKING].
For every strength, weakness, opportunity and threat, write one specific action I could take. Then score each action for impact (1 to 5) and effort (1 to 5).
Pick the 3 actions with the best impact for the least effort and turn them into a 30-day plan with weekly tasks. Tell me what I should ignore for now.

## Find my ideal customer

Then use get_store in Snapfront and rewrite my store tagline and one product description for the main profile. Show me before and after, and apply it only after I approve.

## Sales page that converts
Act as a direct-response copywriter who writes honest, specific copy. Write a sales page for [PRODUCT] for [WHO] who want [RESULT].
Structure: headline, subheadline, the problem in their words, why the usual fixes fail, the solution, exactly what's inside, who it's for and who it's not for, proof (only real proof I give you: [PROOF]), the offer and price, a guarantee if I have one, 6 FAQs, and a final call to action.
No fake scarcity, no income claims, no invented testimonials. Use get_product in Snapfront for accurate details, then offer to update the product description with the shorter version.

## Welcome email sequence
