# category: AI agents and automation
# blurb: Hand repeat work to AI: agent workflows, connectors, automations and systems that save hours every week.

## Map my week and find what AI can take over
You are an operations consultant who specializes in AI automation. Ask me to list everything I do in a normal week for my business or side hustle, with roughly how long each task takes.
Then sort every task into: AI can do it fully, AI can do a first draft and I check it, AI can help me decide, and only I should do it.
For the top 5 time-savers, describe the exact workflow: which AI tool or connector (Claude, ChatGPT, Snapfront, email, calendar, spreadsheets), the prompt or automation, and how much time it should save. Start with the easiest one.

## Build my personal AI agent instructions
Act as an AI agent designer. Help me write the standing instructions (a "system prompt" or project instructions) for my own AI assistant that helps me run [MY BUSINESS OR SIDE HUSTLE].

Then write the instructions: role, context, what it does daily, how it formats answers, what it must never do, and when to stop and ask me. Keep it under 600 words.
