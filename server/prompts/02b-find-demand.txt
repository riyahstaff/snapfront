## Buyer Pain Signal Map
@for: solo sellers exploring a new niche
Act as a commercial researcher and audience-interview designer. I want to build a commercially useful, original resource called 'Buyer Pain Signal Map' for solo sellers exploring a new niche. The specific scope is: map 20 buyer complaints, proof URLs, urgency and willingness-to-pay hypotheses. Use ChatGPT or Claude as a drafting and reasoning partner, not a substitute for real buyer evidence. Ask no more than three essential questions; if I do not answer, proceed using clearly labeled assumptions and sample data.

1. STRATEGY AND RESEARCH: Compare three demand signals that could realistically be checked: buyer search language, community discussions and competing products. Build a source log and label each statement as verified, inferred or untested. Identify the cost of leaving the problem unsolved. If live sources or uploaded files are unavailable, name the evidence needed and never invent citations, interviews, platform metrics or competitor prices.
2. BUILD THE PRODUCT: Deliver a one-page opportunity memo, a 10-question non-leading buyer interview, an evidence table, a three-offer shortlist and a seven-day validation test with stop/go thresholds. Make the output detailed enough that a buyer can complete a real task. Show the exact labels, instructions, examples and success criteria rather than a list of vague suggestions.
3. OFFER AND SNAPFRONT: Turn the findings into a paid niche research report or use them to choose a sellable product; do not misrepresent speculation as sales proof. Draft a short searchable title, a three-sentence value proposition, five product-page benefit bullets, a credible preview idea, and three price points to TEST (not asserted market rates).
4. AGENT OR ROBOT HANDOFF: Identify one repetitive follow-up that a software bot or AI agent could safely do, state the trigger, approved data, human sign-off, log, fallback and measurable time-saving hypothesis. Do not pretend integrations have been installed or executed.
5. QUALITY GATE: Respect privacy, copyright, permissions and applicable marketplace policies. End with a five-step test, the highest-risk assumption, a seven-day buyer-validation action, and the exact files I should export before selling.
