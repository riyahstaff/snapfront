# Premium storefront setup guide

Use **Premium storefront** to set an art-directed look, hero cover and storefront wording in one reviewable flow. It changes the presentation of your store; it does **not** create a new store, change product prices, replace checkout or modify your Snapfront plan or Stripe connection.

## Before you start

- A dedicated staged cover works while your products are still drafts. Without a cover, only photos from live products appear as the automatic fallback on the public storefront and private browser preview.
- Have one clear, wide product or lifestyle photo ready if you want a dedicated hero cover. Portrait photos work, but a wide image usually crops best on the storefront.
- You can leave any hero wording blank. The selected look will use its own fallback wording instead.

## Set up your storefront

1. In the app, open **My store** and tap **Build a premium storefront**. You can also go to **Store look** and tap **Open premium setup**. The five-step setup is saved to this phone for the current store, so it can be resumed later.
2. On **Look**, use the category chips (for example, All premium or the category that matches what you sell). Tap a premium look to select it. This only changes the local setup draft—not your public store.
3. On **Cover**, tap **Choose photo** (or the image area). The system picker normally opens without a separate permission prompt; allow access only if your older device asks. The selected image uploads as a **staged** cover. It does not replace your live cover until you use Apply on the final step.
   - If you do not stage a photo, your existing cover remains in place. If there is no current cover, the storefront can use your newest live product photo as its fallback.
   - Use **Use current** to remove a newly staged choice from this setup draft. Replaced or cleared unused staged uploads are discarded where possible; an applied cover is protected.
   - Select **Still**, **Drift** or **Pan** for the hero image. Drift and pan are small webpage image effects only. They are **not** generated video, true 3D rotation, a new product view or an AI movie.
4. On **Words**, edit the store name and any headline, subheadline, eyebrow, button label, tagline, About text or announcement directly. Manual writing works without AI.
   - To request help, add optional notes and choose a tone, then tap **Get AI suggestions**. The request uses **one photo-to-page allowance**; the screen shows the current allowance meter. Suggestions do not save or publish anything.
   - Review each result and tap its individual **Use** button only for wording you want to copy into the editable fields. Check every claim for accuracy. Do not use AI wording to invent shipping offers, ratings, material facts, certifications, health or medical claims.
5. On **Products**, tap **Add a product from a photo** or **Create a digital product** to use the normal Snapfront product flow. The builder saves step 4 before opening that flow. When you are ready to return, open **My store** and tap **Resume premium storefront**; the product list refreshes and the setup resumes safely. The builder itself never publishes a product—manage a product's draft/live state in its regular product flow.
6. On **Review**, check the local mini-summary of the selected look, staged cover and words. Tap **Preview my storefront** to open a private browser preview.
   - The preview shows all of your staged cover, words and hero motion without applying anything to the public store.
   - It includes only products that are already **live**. Product and store links in the preview navigate to your currently applied public store, not the staged snapshot.
   - The preview link is private, does not save changes, and expires after **30 minutes**. Create another preview from Review if it expires.
7. When ready, tap **Apply to public storefront**. This is the one action that sends your selected look, staged cover reference and text to the public storefront together. It does not publish products and does not alter your existing plan, Stripe connection or checkout.

## Continue later or start over

- Leaving the setup screen is safe. The current store's local draft is restored when you open **Resume premium storefront** from **My store** (or reopen Premium storefront) on the same device.
- Drafts are store-specific. Switching to another store opens that store's own setup draft.
- The saved draft contains short text plus a staged server photo reference—never the photo's base64 image data.
- After a successful Apply, the app clears the local setup draft. If that device-only cleanup ever fails, the app still reports that the storefront was applied; you can reopen the builder and continue or replace the old local draft.

## Need the classic editor instead?

**Store look** is still available for the existing cover photo, About text, announcement and all storefront looks. Use the premium builder when you want the guided five-step review flow; use the classic editor for quick individual edits.
