# 500 digital product ideas — 2026 research

Original niche ideas informed by marketplace categories, paid listings, and 2026 trend reports. This is not a verified top-500 sales ranking. Sources support categories, not sales claims for each idea. Review counts are not sales counts. The year is still in progress.

Researched October 18, 2026. No seller files, designs, reviews or branded listings were copied. No sales volumes or income projections are invented. 420 PDF-oriented ideas and 80 ideas requiring an external working asset. The latter start a clearly labeled PDF companion, not a simulated working asset.
